Paragraph summary:
StudySync is a student-focused web app that helps learners organize their academic work, study more consistently and collaborate with classmates in one shared space. It includes tools like a dashboard, calendar, flashcards, group study area, progress reports, nudge, and a study heatmap, making it useful for students who want to track deadlines, review course material, manage study habit and work with teammates more effectively.
